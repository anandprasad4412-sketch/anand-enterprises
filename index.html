About Anand Enterprises

Founded in 2026, Anand Enterprises is a premier export house based in India. We bridge the gap between India's finest manufacturers and international buyers.

Our expertise spans across six core sectors: Mica, Agricultural Products, Pharmaceuticals, Machinery, Makhana (Fox Nuts), and Cotton. We ensure every product meets global standards before it leaves Indian shores.
